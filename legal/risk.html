Important Risk Warning: Trading financial instruments involves a high degree of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. You could lose some or all of your invested capital. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

1. General Risk Warning

Tradexa is a trading analytics and intelligence platform. It does not constitute investment advice, and nothing in the platform should be interpreted as a recommendation to buy or sell any financial instrument.

Before trading, you should carefully consider your investment objectives, level of experience, and risk appetite. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

2. Market Risk

Financial markets are inherently unpredictable. The value of financial instruments can decrease rapidly due to:

  • Economic conditions and macroeconomic factors
  • Political events and geopolitical risks
  • Central bank policies and interest rate changes
  • Company-specific events and earnings surprises
  • Market sentiment and speculative activity
  • Black swan events and systemic shocks

3. AI and Analytics Limitations

Tradexa's AI Coach and analytics tools are based on historical data and statistical models. These tools have important limitations:

  • Past patterns do not guarantee future performance
  • AI models can make incorrect predictions
  • Backtesting results may differ significantly from live trading due to slippage, liquidity, and market impact
  • Data used in analysis may contain errors or gaps
  • AI recommendations should be considered as one input, not as definitive advice

4. Leveraged Products

If you trade leveraged products (options, futures, forex, CFDs), you should be aware that leverage magnifies both gains and losses. You may lose more than your initial investment.

5. Technology Risk

Use of electronic trading systems carries additional risks including system outages, connectivity failures, data errors, and latency. Tradexa cannot guarantee uninterrupted access to the platform.

6. No Investment Advice

Tradexa is not a regulated investment advisor. Nothing on the platform constitutes personalized investment advice. You should seek advice from a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions, especially if you are uncertain about your risk tolerance or investment objectives.
