What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the service. Cookies can also be stored in your browser's localStorage or sessionStorage — we use these where appropriate as privacy-preserving alternatives to traditional cookies.

Managing Cookies

In the Platform

Use the consent banner (shown on first visit) or go to Settings → Privacy to update your preferences at any time. Your preference is saved locally and respected on every page load.

In Your Browser

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent the platform from working correctly. Common browser cookie settings:

  • Chrome Cookie Settings
  • Firefox Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Safari Cookie Management

Do Not Track

We respect browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. When DNT is enabled in your browser, we disable all optional analytics and marketing cookies automatically, regardless of your cookie consent setting.

Cookie Retention

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies expire on the dates listed in the table above. You can delete all cookies at any time through your browser settings or by resetting preferences using the button above.

Changes to This Policy
