Go to Settings → Integrations → Add Broker. Select your broker from the list of 15+ supported integrations. You'll be redirected to authorize read-only access via OAuth. Trades sync automatically within 2–5 minutes.
Once your broker is connected, Tradexa polls your account every 15 minutes for new closed trades. You can also trigger a manual sync from the Journal page. We only request read-only access and never execute trades.
