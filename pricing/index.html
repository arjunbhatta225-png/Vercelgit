Pricing FAQ

Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
What happens after my free trial ends?
If you started on Free, nothing happens — you stay on Free forever. If you started a 14-day Pro or Elite trial, your account converts to that plan unless you cancel. We send a reminder email 3 days before any trial ends. Cancel anytime with one click.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund.
Is there a discount for annual billing?
Yes! Annual billing saves you 20% — equivalent to getting more than 2 months free. Toggle to annual billing above to see the discounted price.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal. All payments are processed securely through our PCI-compliant payment provider.
Need more than 5 team seats?
Elite includes up to 5 team seats out of the box. If you need a larger team configuration or custom requirements, contact our team and we'll work out a tailored setup.
